## 🚀 Featured project

### 🎮 [Hit the Lights](https://github.com/KristianPol/Hit-the-Lights) · [play it at hitthelights.xyz](https://hitthelights.xyz)

A free rhythm game that runs entirely in the browser. Players upload their own songs, play falling-note charts on four lanes and compete for high scores, with no download required.

**My role:** Backend Lead, Design & Security, in a team of three with Alex Mukhin (DevOps, global infrastructure and chart tooling) and Aniket Tripathi (UI/UX design).

**Gameplay**
- Four-lane gameplay with precise timing windows and responsive keyboard input
- Easy to Expert difficulties for each song, plus custom chart uploads
- Live HUD showing accuracy, combo and max combo
- Four score ranks: Radiant, Shining, Glimmer and Shattered
- Remappable controls and note fall speed from 0.5x to 2.5x

**Community**
- Public and private song library with cover art
- Global leaderboards per difficulty, plus personal bests
- Friend requests, in-game messaging, score sharing, song likes and comments
- Achievements in Skill, Progression and Social categories, which can be pinned to a profile
- Custom profile pictures, playtime tracking and analytics
- Guest play, with progress that syncs on registration
- Six UI themes, including a light mode

**Stack:** Angular 21, TypeScript and SCSS on the frontend; Node.js and Express on the backend; Neon (serverless PostgreSQL) for data; Cloudflare R2 for storage; hosted on Render.

### 🗺️ [EuropaMap](https://github.com/KristianPol/EuropaMap)

A team school project with Ostap Malanyak and Oleksandr Mukhin. It started as a clickable map of Europe, where each country leads to a page with facts such as its capital and population, along with its flag, photos and a Wikipedia link.

We then added a web application, first planned as a productivity tool and later reworked into a **travel planner**.
